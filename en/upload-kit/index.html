Good to know before you upload

    
  • The start of the title matters
    Search results and phones show only the first part. Put the key words first.
  • Keep to 3 hashtags
    Only the first 3 show above the title, and too many can be ignored. Genre, theme and type are enough.
  • Keep chapter names short
    Long chapter names get cut off. Shorten the track names.
  • Keep thumbnail text short
    It must read on a small screen. Around ten characters per line works well.

  


  

    
FAQ

    
Is it really free? Are my files uploaded?
It is free and needs no account. Audio files are never sent to a server. The page only reads each file’s name and length in your browser.
How do YouTube chapters work?
Put a list like "0:00 Song name" in the description. It must start at 0:00, have at least 3 entries, and each part must be 10 seconds or longer.
I will merge the songs. Will the times match?
If you added a pause or an overlap when merging, enter the same values in Pro mode and the timestamps will match. You can also compare with the merge tool’s own list.
Do I have to add the "made with AI" line?
I can’t say it is required, so it is optional. YouTube Studio asks about altered or synthetic content when you upload, so check that question yourself.
Is my progress saved?
In-progress work is saved temporarily in this browser so a refresh does not lose it. The audio files are not saved, only names and lengths.
