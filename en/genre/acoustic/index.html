Acoustic & Folk Prompt Tips
The same prompt can give very different results depending on how you use it. Remember these four things.

    
  • Keep it to one or two instruments
    The more instruments you add, the less acoustic it feels. One guitar plus a harmonica or strings is plenty.
  • Use 'minimal production'
    Add this phrase for a sparse arrangement. It helps prevent over-arranging.
  • Describe a concrete scene
    A scene like 'saying goodbye to an old friend' gives the song a clear emotion.
  • Choose one vocal tone
    Warm male or breathy female: pick one.

    
Can I put Korean lyrics in an acoustic song?
Yes. Keep the style box in English and put the Korean lyrics in the lyrics box.
What is the difference between folk and acoustic pop?
Folk leans toward understated storytelling; acoustic pop has a clearer chorus. Compare the tempo and mood on each card.
Do I have to use the 'avoid:' part?
No. On Pro/Premier you can paste it into the Exclude Styles box in advanced options; otherwise just leave it at the end of the style box. The card's 'Copy exclusions' button gives you the parts separately.
