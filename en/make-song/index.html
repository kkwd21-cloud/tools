    Good to know for your first song

    • One mood per song

      Mixing sad and upbeat words confuses Suno. The check step flags clashing words.

    • Leave artist names out

      Real artist names are often rejected or ignored. Describing the sound in words is safer.

    • Keep the chorus identical

      If the chorus changes every time, the song feels scattered. The check step can match them in one click.

    • Not liking the first result is normal

      Run the same prompt a few times and keep the best. Logging results makes the next pick easier.

    FAQ

    Is it really free? Do I need an account?

    It is free and needs no account. What you type is never sent to a server. Everything runs inside your browser.

    What is the difference between Beginner and Pro mode?

    Beginner shows only choices and buttons. Pro mode adds BPM and key input, song structure, hint options, direct style editing, your own lyrics, and jumping between steps.

    Can I check lyrics I wrote myself?

    Yes. Turn on Pro mode in step 3 and use the manual edit mode, or paste straight into the lyrics box in step 4.

    Will Suno make exactly what I planned?

    No. Suno gives different results from the same text. This tool reduces mistakes and gives you a clear order to work in.

    Is my progress saved?

    In-progress work is saved temporarily in this browser so a refresh does not lose it. The log in step 5 is saved only when you press the button.
