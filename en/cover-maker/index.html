Tips

  • Start with the mood

    Choosing a mood sets color and picture together so they match. Then change one thing at a time.

  • Press Shuffle a few times

    The same style changes its clouds, hills and lights every press. Keep pressing until you like one.

  • Keep the title short

    A long title becomes small and hard to read. Check the preview at phone size.

  • For AI art, ask for no text

    AI often garbles text inside pictures. The prompts already ask for no text - add your title here.

  • Save all three sizes

    Get cover, YouTube and Shorts sizes in one ZIP so you do not have to rebuild later.

FAQ

Which AI can I use the prompts with?

Any text-to-image AI such as Midjourney, ChatGPT, Gemini, Stable Diffusion or Flux. The format differs by AI, so there is a tab for each.

Can I use AI images as an album cover?

Each AI service has its own rules for commercial use. Check the terms of the service that made the picture. This site does not give legal advice.

Can I freely use the pictures made here?

The backgrounds and text are drawn by this page itself, with no outside images or AI. Fonts are Google Fonts under open licenses.

What sizes are saved?

Album cover 3000×3000, YouTube 1920×1080, Shorts and Reels 1080×1920. On weaker devices a large image may be saved a little smaller.

My text is cut off or overlaps.

Try another layout or reduce the text size under Fine-tune. Long titles are wrapped and shrunk automatically.

Can I add text to my own image?

Yes. Under "Fancier with AI" in step 2, add your image and we put the text on top. It can be a photo you took, not only AI art.

Is my image uploaded?

No. This is a static page with no server, so nothing is sent anywhere.
