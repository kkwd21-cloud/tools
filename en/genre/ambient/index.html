Ambient & Instrumental Prompt Tips
The same prompt can give very different results depending on how you use it. Remember these four things.

    
  • Use 'no drums' and 'tempo-free'
    Add these for music that flows without a beat. For ambient, describe space and texture instead of tempo.
  • Turn on Instrumental, leave lyrics empty
    Ambient usually has no vocals. Clearing the lyrics and enabling Instrumental in Suno is the most reliable approach.
  • Pick one scene
    'Floating in still water' or 'walking through morning fog': one scene keeps the mood consistent.
  • Chain tracks for long videos
    For sleep or focus videos, long runtimes are usually built by joining several tracks.

    
Does an ambient track still need a title?
Yes. Even without vocals, the title conveys the mood. Try the title generator on this page.
How do I make a sleep track?
Collect slow, soft phrases like 'very slow soft pads', 'no rhythm', and 'deep sleep and calm mood'.
Do I have to use the 'avoid:' part?
No. For ambient, listing 'drums' in the exclusions helps prevent a sudden beat.
