Cinematic & Epic Prompt Tips
The same prompt can give very different results depending on how you use it. Remember these four things.

    
  • Describe the dynamics start to finish
    Something like 'builds from quiet strings to massive climax' brings the trailer tension to life.
  • Be specific about instruments
    Choir, brass, timpani: the more specific the instruments, the clearer the orchestral feel.
  • Say so if you don't want vocals
    If you want pure orchestra and no choir, add 'instrumental, no vocals'.
  • Keep one central mood word
    Heroic, dark, or bittersweet: build around one mood word.

    
How long do trailer-style songs run?
It varies per song. If you need longer, generate several times and edit the sections you like together.
Can I name a film score?
Use general phrases like 'orchestra that starts quiet and swells huge' instead of naming a specific work or artist.
Do I have to use the 'avoid:' part?
No. On Pro/Premier you can paste it into the Exclude Styles box in advanced options; otherwise just leave it at the end of the style box. The card's 'Copy exclusions' button gives you the parts separately.
