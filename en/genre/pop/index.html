K-Pop & Pop Prompt Tips
The same prompt can give very different results depending on how you use it. Remember these four things.

    
  • State the tempo clearly
    Tempo defines a pop song's character. Try 100 to 130 bpm depending on the energy you want.
  • Imagine the chorus first
    A phrase like 'catchy pre-chorus into anthemic drop chorus' makes a clear hook more likely.
  • Pick one vocal setup
    Female group, male solo, or duet: choosing one keeps results stable.
  • Generate a few and keep the best hook
    Hook quality varies a lot even with the same settings. Pull two or three more and compare.

    
How do I make a Korean-lyrics pop song?
Write the style box in English and put Korean lyrics in the lyrics box. The lyrics generator on this page sets up the structure too.
How do I get a K-pop feel?
Combine genre and structure keywords such as 'kpop', 'catchy pre-chorus', and 'drop chorus' to get closer.
Do I have to use the 'avoid:' part?
No. On Pro/Premier you can paste it into the Exclude Styles box in advanced options; otherwise just leave it at the end of the style box. The card's 'Copy exclusions' button gives you the parts separately.
