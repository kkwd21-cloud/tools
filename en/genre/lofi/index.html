Lo-Fi & Chill Prompt Tips
The same prompt can give very different results depending on how you use it. Remember these four things.

    
  • Use only one or two textures
    Vinyl crackle, tape hiss, rain: one or two is enough. Too many make the sound muddy.
  • Keep tempo between 70 and 90 bpm
    That range is where the relaxed lo-fi feel comes out best. Use the Remix button on a card to try other tempos.
  • Turn on Instrumental for no vocals
    If you want no vocals at all, Suno's Instrumental option is the most reliable switch.
  • Chain several songs for long videos
    A single song is short, so long videos are usually built from several tracks in a row.

    
Can I add lyrics to lo-fi?
Yes, but lo-fi is mostly instrumental. Short humming or spoken-word lines fit best.
How do I make a one-hour video?
Most people collect several songs into a playlist video. An editing tool like MusicCanvas lets you arrange the track order and background in one go.
Do I have to use the 'avoid:' part?
No. On Pro/Premier you can paste it into the Exclude Styles box in advanced options; otherwise just leave it at the end of the style box. The card's 'Copy exclusions' button gives you the parts separately.
