    Ways to use it

    • Match the vibe of a song you like

      Add a file you own, then make a new song from a prompt in the same tempo range. Do not use artist names or lyrics.

    • Recreate your own Suno song

      Check the BPM and flow of a song you made in Suno, then generate more with a similar feel.

    • Lyrics too

      Paste the structure template in the Lyrics box, then put lyrics from the lyrics helper under each tag.

    Tips

    • Pick a genre if you know it

      If Auto is not what you want, press a genre to get matches only from that genre.

    • Choose a vocal type

      Pick female, male, duet or instrumental and prompts with that vocal come first.

    • Fix odd BPM right away

      A slow song can come out at double speed. Tap along, and if it does not match, press Halve or Double.

    • Listen to all three

      The same song gives different results with each prompt. Make each one and pick your favorite.

    • Copyright

      When you make a Suno song from someone else's track, do not copy the artist name, lyrics or melody. This tool only uses tempo and mood.

    • Results are a guide

      Section names, BPM and key are estimated from the sound and can be wrong.

    Questions

    Is this song analyzer free?

    Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and all analysis happens in your browser.

    Is my audio uploaded to a server?

    No. This site is a static page with no server, so your files are never sent anywhere.

    How are the similar prompts chosen?

    It calculates the song's BPM, loudness, brightness and major or minor feel, then compares them with the tempo and mood words in each prompt. It is a calculation, not an AI writing names or lyrics.

    Does it make titles and lyrics too?

    This tool does analysis and prompt matching. Lyrics can be made in the lyrics helper from a theme and genre, and you can use them with the structure template.

    Are the BPM and sections accurate?

    They are estimated from the sound and can be wrong. BPM sometimes comes out double or half, so there are buttons to fix it.

    Which files can I use?

    Any file your browser can play, such as mp3, wav, m4a, ogg or flac.

    Can I use a YouTube link?

    No. It needs an audio file, so add a file you own.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Short songs work, but long ones can run out of memory. A computer is recommended.
